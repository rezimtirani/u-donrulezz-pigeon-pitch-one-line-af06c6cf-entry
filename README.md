One elevator pitch from a city pigeon to investors: a $2M seed round to disrupt the breadcrumb supply chain — 40 million pigeons, zero churn, and every statue in the city already a customer.

> We're raising a $2M seed round to disrupt the breadcrumb supply chain: 40 million pigeons, zero churn, and every statue in the city is already a customer.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the pitch to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line, and not only whitespace.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
